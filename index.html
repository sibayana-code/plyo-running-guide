Short guide

Plyometrics train the stretch–shortening cycle — the tendon “spring,” especially Achilles — so you spend less energy at a given pace. They work best with your running and your RIR 2–3 lifting, after weeks of clean contacts, not as a replacement for either.

Principles

  1. Fresh first. Plyos before hard running, or on a separate day — never after tempo, intervals, or a long run. Easy jog after a short L/M block is OK.
  2. Quality over junk volume. Short ground contact, quiet stiff landings, soft knees. Stop when contacts get loud, slow, or sticky.
  3. Progress slowly. Tendons adapt slower than muscle. Cap weekly contact increases around 10%.
  4. Soft surface early (grass, rubber). Supportive shoes. No concrete depth jumps while learning.
  5. Your lifting at RIR 2–3 is the green light for Zone L–M. Not yet for max depth jumps.

Intensity gauge

ZoneWhat it feels likeGround contact
L LowQuiet landings, little flight, bilateral or skipSoft, controlled
M MedNoticeable bounce, short amortizationCrisp “tap–pop”
H HighDrop / single-leg / continuous reactiveVery short; RSI-style

Amortization = time from landing to push-off (keep it short). RSI-style = reactive strength: high bounce for how long you’re on the ground. Aim high intent (~80–100% of that drill’s crisp max), not grinding fatigue. If height or pop drops ~10% in a set, end the plyo block.

  • Sample (~50): pl-ankling 2×10 + pl-pogo 2×10 + pl-line 2×5

Stop when…

  • Knees cave, heels slam, or contacts get long and sticky
  • Sharp Achilles / patellar pain (not normal DOMS)
  • Planned contacts done or quality gone — whichever first

What a good contact feels like

  • Quiet feet. Stiff ankles. Soft knees that don’t collapse inward.
  • Short time on the ground — “tap–pop,” not a sticky squat.
  • You could do a few more crisp reps. When pop dies, the set is over.

How to progress

  • Follow next: on each card (same idea as the push-up ladder).
  • Two clean weeks of Zone L → start Zone M at pl-pogocont.
  • Zone H only when pain-free and landings stay quiet for months of L/M.
  • Failed / loud set: that set does not count — drop to the easier ID or cut contacts next time.
  • Film a set when you can. Loud landings = easier ID.

Pairing with runs

  • Easy run: plyo then easy, or easy after short L/M — not after a hard day
  • Tempo / intervals: plyo before if short and fresh, or separate day — never after
  • Long run: no Zone H same day; L only if fresh
  • Heavy lower lift: ≥24–48 h from Zone H
  • Race week: cut volume 30–50%, or skip
