Plyo for running

Tendon spring for runners. Match the ID in the Gym log. Start Zone L.

Short guide

Plyometrics train the stretch–shortening cycle — the tendon “spring,” especially Achilles — so you spend less energy at a given pace. They work best with your running and your RIR 2–3 lifting, after weeks of clean contacts, not as a replacement for either.

Principles

  1. Fresh first. Plyos before a run, or on a separate day — never after a hard session.
  2. Quality over junk volume. Short ground contact, quiet stiff landings, soft knees. Stop when contacts get loud, slow, or sticky.
  3. Progress slowly. Tendons adapt slower than muscle. Cap weekly contact increases around 10%.
  4. Soft surface early (grass, rubber). Supportive shoes. No concrete depth jumps while learning.
  5. Your lifting at RIR 2–3 is the green light for Zone L–M. Not yet for max depth jumps.

H HighDrop / single-leg / continuous reactiveVery short; RSI-style

Aim high intent on each drill (~80–100% of that drill’s crisp max), not grinding fatigue. If height or pop drops ~10% in a set, end the plyo block.

Quantity gauge

Count foot contacts (each landing = 1).

  • Start (weeks 1–2): 40–60 contacts @ Zone L only
  • Build: 60–80 before adding Zone M
  • NSCA bands (ceiling, not a target): beginner 80–100 · intermediate 100–120 · advanced 120–140 per session
  • Runner-practical: often 30–100 quality contacts is enough if form holds

Sets / reps

  • L: 2–3 × 8–12 contacts (or 2–3 × 20–30s continuous pogos)
  • M: 3–4 × 4–8 contacts
  • H: 3–5 × 3–6 contacts

Rest: ~60–120+ s between high sets (about 1:5 to 1:10 work:rest). Walk; don’t gas.

Frequency: 1–2×/week. 48–72 h between plyo sessions. Don’t stack hard legs + Zone H + tempo in the same 24 h.

Stop when…

  • Knees cave, heels slam, or contacts get long and sticky
  • Sharp Achilles / patellar pain (not normal DOMS)
  • Planned contacts done or quality gone — whichever first

What a good contact feels like

  • Quiet feet. Stiff ankles. Soft knees that don’t collapse inward.
  • Short time on the ground — “tap–pop,” not a sticky squat.
  • You could do a few more crisp reps. When pop dies, the set is over.

How to progress

  • Two clean weeks of Zone L → unlock Zone M.
  • Zone H only when pain-free and landings stay quiet for months of L/M.
  • Film a set when you can. Loud landings = easier ID or fewer contacts.

Pairing with runs

  • Easy run: OK after short L/M, or plyo then easy
  • Tempo / intervals: plyo before if short and fresh, or separate day — never after
  • Long run: no Zone H same day; L only if fresh
  • Heavy lower lift: ≥24–48 h from Zone H
  • Race week: cut volume 30–50%, or skip

Catalog

Easier to harder. Intensity tag + suggested contacts per set.

pl-ankling Ankling L

Fast feet in place, short stiff ankles. Little flight. Teaches stiffness without big impact.

2–3 × 8–12 contacts (or 20–30s)

pl-pogo Low pogo hops L

Bilateral, small amplitude, quiet landings. Stay tall through the ankles.

2–3 × 8–12 contacts

pl-skip Light A-skip L

Rhythm + stiffness. Soft contacts, not a bound yet.

2–3 × 20–30s or 8–12 contacts/side

pl-line Line hops L

Bilateral hops forward–back or side–side over a line. Low height, quiet feet.

2–3 × 8–12 contacts

pl-squatstick Squat jump to stick L

Jump up, land and pause. Teaches absorb before rebound. Soft knees, no slam.

2–3 × 6–8 contacts

pl-pogocont Continuous pogos M

Higher intent, still bilateral. Short amortization — bounce, don’t squat.

3–4 × 6–10 contacts · unlock after 2 clean Zone L weeks

pl-box Low box jump M

Jump onto ≤20–30 cm box, step down. Soft stick on the box.

3–4 × 4–6 contacts

pl-hurdle Mini-hurdle hops M

Bilateral continuous hops over low cones/hurdles. Keep contacts short.

3–4 × 4–8 contacts

pl-bound Short alternating bound M

Short distance alternating leaps. Control the landing before the next push.

3–4 × 4–6 contacts/side

pl-slpogo Single-leg pogo (low) M

Low single-leg hops. Only after bilateral pogos are quiet and crisp.

3 × 4–6 contacts/side

pl-depth Depth / drop jump H

Step off ≤20–30 cm, rebound up. Increase height only if contact stays short. Advanced.

3–5 × 3–5 contacts · months of clean L/M first

pl-slbound Single-leg bound H

Hops for distance on one leg. High intensity — keep volume tiny.

3–5 × 3–5 contacts/side
